# Privacy Policy - LongSS Extension

**Last Updated**: July 23, 2026

## Overview

LongSS is committed to protecting your privacy. This document explains how the extension handles your data.

## Data Collection

**LongSS does NOT collect, store, or transmit any user data.**

### What We DON'T Collect:
- ❌ Personal information
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Captured screenshots
- ❌ Website content
- ❌ Usage analytics
- ❌ Tracking cookies
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Device information

## How the Extension Works

### Local Processing Only
- All screenshot capture happens on your device
- Image processing is done locally using browser APIs
- No external servers are involved
- No data leaves your computer

### Permissions Explained

LongSS requires the following browser permissions:

#### 1. `activeTab`
**Why**: To capture screenshots of the page you're viewing
**What it does**: Allows temporary access to the current tab when you click the extension
**What it doesn't do**: Cannot access tabs you haven't explicitly used the extension on

#### 2. `storage`
**Why**: To save your extension preferences (future feature)
**What it does**: Stores settings locally in your browser
**What it doesn't do**: Does not sync or upload any data to external servers

#### 3. `downloads`
**Why**: To save captured screenshots to your computer
**What it does**: Triggers the browser's download functionality
**What it doesn't do**: Does not upload files anywhere

#### 4. `scripting`
**Why**: To inject capture scripts for scroll recording
**What it does**: Adds temporary code to pages for scroll detection
**What it doesn't do**: Does not modify page content or behavior permanently

#### 5. `<all_urls>` (Host Permissions)
**Why**: To work on any website you choose
**What it does**: Allows the extension to capture screenshots on any site
**What it doesn't do**: Does not monitor or track your browsing

## Data Storage

### Local Storage
- Extension preferences are stored locally in your browser
- No data is synchronized or backed up externally
- Clearing browser data removes all extension data

### Screenshots
- Screenshots are saved directly to your Downloads folder
- Files remain only on your device
- Extension does not retain copies
- You have full control over saved files
